# RequestBridge
### Your words. A clearer public records request.

RequestBridge is an **AI-powered** assistant that helps people turn everyday questions into clear, editable Florida public records requests.

## The Problem
People often know what information they want but do not know how to describe the government records they need. This can create confusion between requesters and agency staff.

## How It Works
1. Describe what you are looking for in your own words.
2. Clarify your request through helpful questions and suggested record types.
3. Review and edit a draft before sharing it with the agency.

## Project Goal
Help requesters and government agencies understand each other while keeping the requester in control of the final wording.

## Built With
- Archie
- AI-assisted request clarification and drafting

## About
A solo project for the eMerge AI Hackathon 2026 — Enterprise AI track, inspired by hands-on records-management experience.

## Disclaimer
RequestBridge is an independent prototype, not a government service or legal advice provider. It does not determine exemptions, guarantee access to records, or automatically submit requests. Review all generated content before use.
